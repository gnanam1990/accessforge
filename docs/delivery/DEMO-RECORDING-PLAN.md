# Evidence-gated demo recording plan

**Recording plan only. No video or actual-reader acceptance is claimed by this file.**
Target length: 4 minutes 40 seconds, leaving margin below five minutes. Record only an
authorized test target and consented private evidence; exclude credentials and unrelated
desktop/account content. Use English narration or provide an English translation.

| Time | Screen and narration | Required evidence before using the segment |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Explain the form-error recovery problem and the engineer/reviewer audience. | Describe a concrete test journey, not an invented customer incident. |
| 0:25–0:55 | Show the authorized project, frozen journey and exact runtime profile. | Actual configuration; distinguish target profile from qualified profile. |
| 0:55–1:45 | Show the real baseline interaction and its retained failure evidence. | Actual VoiceOver capture, original run identity and finalized baseline. |
| 1:45–2:35 | Show diagnosis, constrained source diff and human patch approval. | Original model operation, retained proposal and real approval record. |
| 2:35–3:35 | Show the candidate build and independent repeated journey. | Fresh candidate execution, matched identities and protected functional results. |
| 3:35–4:10 | Show review and an independently verified evidence export. | Real reviewer decision and verification output with its trust limitations. |
| 4:10–4:40 | Summarize what the recording establishes and what remains unsupported. | Exact revision/profile; no broad accessibility or production-readiness claim. |

## Capture rules

- If a required segment has no real evidence, do not narrate it in the past tense or recreate
  it with synthetic data. A development walkthrough must be labelled as such and is not a
  substitute for a working actual-reader demonstration.
- Keep enough continuous interaction to show cause and result. Any time compression or
  omitted build wait must be labelled. Do not splice unrelated baseline and candidate runs.
- Keep credentials, cookies, private model inputs and unrelated desktop content out of capture.
  Review the full video before publication; a failed capture does not authorize a live replay.
- Do not turn on the reader, invoke a paid model, upload a video or submit an entry merely
  because this recording plan exists. Those steps retain their explicit authorization gates.
